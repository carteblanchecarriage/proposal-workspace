03

Build your fee, not another spreadsheet

Most studios' fees live in a spreadsheet that's been copied, tweaked and emailed around for years. One wrong cell and the total is off. Here the worksheet does the math for you:

  • No broken formulas. Hours × rates by phase and role from programming through CA, your consultants with your markup, and every total worked out the same way, every time.
  • One fee per project, shared. No "Fee_v7_FINAL.xlsx": the whole team opens the same, current fee.
  • A built-in sanity check. Your bottom-up fee is compared with the percent of construction you'd expect for a project this size, from your own fee curve.
  • Ready for the proposal. A clean fee table by phase and consultant, to copy straight in. Or let AI draft the hours from the RFP.

05

Change it by asking

Fee and schedule each have an assistant. Tell it what changed, the way you'd tell your PM, and it edits the worksheet or the schedule for you, keeping every total and date in step. One Undo takes it back.

  • Fees: add or remove tasks, adjust hours, change the target, markup or expenses. Your billing rates stay yours.
  • Schedules: shift phases, add reviews and permitting, set milestones, and the legend follows.
Add weekly design meetings through DD, 1.5 hours for the PM and project architect.

Tuned to your firm, not generic AI

Tell it once what kind of practice you are and how you decide. Every tool reads it, so a go/no-go, a fee draft or an answer in chat sounds like your firm, not a template.

  • Your judgment. The project types and clients you want, your red flags and deal-breakers (contract terms, fee-heavy scoring), how you weigh fee against fit, and where the market is.
  • Your numbers. The fee you expect at each construction budget, your split by phase, and your usual proposal sections.
  • Your track record. Add past pursuits, won or lost, with the debrief. The app checks your world view against how they turned out and suggests changes.
