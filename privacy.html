Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Proposal Workspace is operated by Proposal Workspace, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Proposal Workspace website and app (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. The Service is in an early, invite-only beta, and this policy may change as it develops.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and password (stored by our authentication provider; we never see your password), and the name of your organization.
  • Your content. The documents you upload (such as RFPs and consultant proposals) and what you create in the Service: RFP reviews, proposal comparisons, projects, consultants, schedules, fees and your organization's World view.
  • Access requests. If you request access, the name, email, firm and note you send us.
  • Feedback. Messages you send with the Feedback button, with your email address.
  • Usage information. For each AI feature you run, the feature, the amount of AI processing used and its cost, linked to your organization. We use this to apply usage limits and, for paid plans, billing.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical data such as IP addresses and browser type in server logs. We may use cookie-free, aggregate analytics (page views and referrers) to understand how the site is used.
  • Payment information. If you subscribe, payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and billing details from Stripe, but never your full card number.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: to sign you in, store your work, and run the features you choose.
  • To process documents with AI when you run an AI feature, as described below.
  • To review access requests and respond to feedback.
  • To apply usage limits, bill paid plans, prevent abuse, and keep the Service secure and working.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use your content for advertising.

AI processing

When you run an AI feature (for example, reviewing an RFP, comparing proposals, or asking an assistant), the relevant documents and records are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the result. This only happens when you run a feature. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this content is not used to train AI models, and we don't use it to train models either. AI output can be wrong or incomplete; see our Terms.

How your content is stored and protected

  • Your saved work (RFP reviews, comparisons, projects, consultants, schedules, fees and World view) is encrypted before it's stored, with a key specific to your organization.
  • Each organization's content is kept separate: other organizations can't see it, and in the Service only people signed in to that organization can open it.
  • Connections to the Service are encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Because the Service must decrypt your content to show it to you and to run the features you choose, Proposal Workspace, LLC holds the encryption keys. We don't access your content except to provide the Service, to help you when you ask us to, or where required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Your saved work is kept until you delete it, or until your organization is deleted.
  • Uploaded RFP PDFs are kept for up to 30 days, so page references in a review can open the original, and then deleted. Other uploads that pass through our storage are deleted as soon as they've been processed, and in any case within a day.
  • Copies held by our AI provider for processing are deleted after about a day.
  • Access requests and feedback are kept while they're useful to us, and deleted on request.

Who processes your information

We use these service providers to run the Service. Each only receives what it needs for its part:

  • Anthropic: AI processing.
  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Vercel: hosting.
  • Stripe: payments, when you subscribe.
  • An email provider, to send sign-in, invite and password emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to it.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. To keep you signed in, the Service stores your sign-in session in your browser's storage; the demo keeps its sample data in your browser tab only. Any analytics we use are cookie-free.

Your choices and rights

  • Export. Anyone in your organization can download all of its saved data from the Organization page.
  • Delete. You can delete individual records at any time, and the owner can delete the whole organization and everything in it.
  • Access, correction and deletion of your account. Ask us using the Feedback button while signed in, or through Request access on our home page, and we'll help.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We'll honor them as required.

Children

The Service is meant for professionals and isn't directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change, and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let account holders know.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach us with the Feedback button while signed in, or through Request access on our home page.
